🌙 Night Priority & Flow

1️⃣ 🛡️ Soldier Protection
Soldiers choose a player to protect.

A player may night be protected twice the same night in a row.

Players do not know they were protected or the werewolves choose not to kill.

2️⃣ 🐺 Werewolf Attack
Werewolves simultaneously choose a player to attack.

If multiple are selected, one is choosen at random.

Werewolves may choose no one.

If the target is protected, the attack fails.

3️⃣ 🔮 Seer Investigation
The seer investigates a player and receives information: yes or no if they are a non-villager.
If the seer is killed and not protected, they receive no investigation result that night.
